Help Center & FAQ

Everything you need to know before, during, and after your Tesra journey.

Bookings & Payments

You can book through our Tours page using the booking request form, or call us directly at +254 700 123 456. A consultant will confirm availability and send you a detailed itinerary and payment link within 24 hours.

We accept M-Pesa, Visa, Mastercard, bank transfers (KES and USD), and PayPal. A 30% deposit is required to secure your booking, with the balance due 30 days before departure.

All listed prices are per person, based on twin-share accommodation. Single supplement fees apply for solo travellers wishing to occupy a private room — please ask your consultant for the exact supplement when requesting a quote.

Cancellations & Changes

  • 60+ days before departure: Full refund minus 5% admin fee
  • 30–59 days before departure: 50% refund
  • 15–29 days before departure: 25% refund
  • Under 15 days: No refund (travel insurance strongly recommended)

Date changes are permitted up to 45 days before departure, subject to availability. A date-change fee of $75 per person applies. Changes within 45 days are treated as cancellations under our standard policy.

In the rare event we cancel a tour (e.g., natural disaster, government travel advisory), you will receive a full refund within 10 business days, or the option to transfer to another tour of equal value at no extra cost.

Visas & Documents

Yes. Our visa assistance service covers 40+ destinations including Schengen, UK, USA, Japan, and India. We prepare your documentation checklist, review your application, and liaise with embassies. Visa fees are separate from tour costs.

We recommend beginning your visa application at least 8–12 weeks before departure for Schengen and other complex applications. Some destinations offer e-visas with 48-hour processing. Your consultant will advise on your specific destination's timeline.

Travel Insurance

Travel insurance is mandatory for all Tesra tours. We include a standard policy (medical, evacuation, trip interruption) in all our packages. You may upgrade to a comprehensive plan covering adventure sports and pre-existing conditions for a small additional premium.

Our standard policy covers most trekking activities up to 5,000m altitude. For high-altitude climbs (Everest Base Camp, Kilimanjaro summit), white-water rafting, and skydiving, an adventure sports upgrade is required. We'll confirm which activities need the upgrade during your booking consultation.

Our team is available Monday–Saturday, 8am–7pm EAT. We typically respond within 2 hours.
