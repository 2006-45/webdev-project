Our Story

Eighteen years of turning wanderlust into unforgettable reality — one carefully crafted journey at a time.

Who We Are

Passion-Driven Travel Since 2006

Tesra was founded by Nairobi-based adventurers Fatima Wanjiku and James Ochieng after a life-changing backpacking trip across Southeast Asia. Their mission was simple: make transformative travel accessible, responsible, and deeply personal.

Today, our 40-person team operates from our Westlands headquarters, partnering with trusted local guides and eco-lodges across 85+ destinations worldwide. We believe travel has the power to build empathy, support local economies, and create memories that outlast any material possession.

What Drives Us

Our Core Values

Sustainability

We offset 100% of carbon emissions, partner with green-certified lodges, and contribute 2% of revenue to conservation projects.

Community

70% of our local guides and accommodation partners are community-owned, ensuring tourism dollars stay in destination economies.

Excellence

We hold ourselves to the highest standard. Every itinerary is reviewed by our senior consultant team before being shared with a client.

Authenticity

We favour real local experiences over tourist traps — home-cooked meals, artisan workshops, and off-the-beaten-path gems.

Our Journey

18 Years of Milestones

2006
The Beginning

Fatima and James register Tesra from a small office in Nairobi's CBD. First three tours: Zanzibar, Masai Mara, and Victoria Falls.

2010
International Expansion

Launched European and Asian tour routes. Moved to a larger Westlands office. Team grows to 12 full-time consultants.

2014
Sustainability Pledge

Signed the Travelife sustainability commitment. Partnered with 20 community-led lodges across Africa and Asia.

2018
Digital Transformation

Launched our online booking platform. Reached 5,000 happy travellers milestone. Won Best East Africa Agency award for the first time.

2022
Post-Pandemic Relaunch

Rebuilt with new flexible booking policies and a 100% carbon offset programme. Team expanded to 40 specialists.

2026
Today

12,000+ travellers served. 85+ destinations. Three-time Best Travel Agency – East Africa. Still growing, still inspired.
